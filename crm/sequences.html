State 1 · Sequences: cards with touches and active contacts, and how each sequence performs; live — edit, add touches, delete

Sequences

A sequence is a follow-up cadence: calls, voicemails and emails spread over working days. When a rep first calls a contact, the contact is enrolled in the default sequence, and each day's touches appear as tasks on the rep's Tasks page.

Performance

How contacts are moving through each sequence. Completion = completed ÷ (completed + exited).

Sequence Active Completed Exited ↳ Exited, Meeting ↳ Exited, Deal ↳ Exited, Manual Total Completion

Exited means a contact left the sequence early. ↳ Meeting, ↳ Deal and ↳ Manual split it by reason: a meeting booked, a deal won or lost, or removed by a rep.
